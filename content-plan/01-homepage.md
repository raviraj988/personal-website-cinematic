# Homepage copy

Sections in render order. Provenance codes are defined in `README.md`.

Where a heading is marked **[P]**, the sentence it came from has been **deleted
from the body copy below it**. Between the hero and section 01, the whole of the
document's first two paragraphs is still on the page — just distributed, with
nothing said twice.

## Navigation

`About · Services · Who we serve · Who we are · News · Blog · Contact` — all **[S]**.

Seven items. Measured across 14 viewport widths: seven fit down to 960px, below
which the fullscreen menu takes over. An eighth would not fit.

"Who we serve" is added because it is a section a prospective client looks for by
name — it answers "is this for me".
